About this platform

Tools for the Tough Days is a digital wellbeing and habit-building platform. It provides structured self-awareness tools, psychoeducational resources, and habit-anchoring support grounded in clinical methodology.

Operated by Nic Marcon, Registered Psychologist (AHPRA No. PSY0000962669). All resources are clinically authored and reviewed before publication.

This platform does not provide

  • Psychological treatment, diagnosis, or a therapeutic relationship
  • A substitute for professional clinical care
  • Personalised clinical advice
  • Crisis or emergency mental health support

Need counselling?

In-house counselling services are offered separately under their own service agreement. Accessing this platform does not constitute entry into a counselling relationship.
