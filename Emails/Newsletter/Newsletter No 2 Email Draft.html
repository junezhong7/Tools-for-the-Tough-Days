You already know what you should be doing

Almost nobody I sit with is short on information.

They know they should be sleeping better, moving more, stopping earlier. They have read it all. Knowing was never the problem.

There is a lot of research on this, and it keeps landing in the same place. Properly deciding to do something only gets you about halfway to actually doing it.

In the studies that follow people day by day, the ones who keep it going all did the same thing. They tied it to something that already happens without them thinking about it, like making a cuppa, finishing work, or first thing in the morning. Then they did it often enough that it stopped being a decision.

So the thing worth trying this fortnight is not a better plan. It is a smaller one.

The one thing

Pick something you can do in under two minutes, and attach it to something you already do every day. Then do it on the days you do not feel like it. Those are the days it is built on.
